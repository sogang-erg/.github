## About

ERG studies how immersive experiences work, and how to make them work better. From empirical studies of XR presence and perception to real-time 3D and neural rendering, we use games as our research platform to build experiences that feel vivid, comfortable, and shared.

## Research

**Extended Reality (XR)**
When does a virtual moment feel real? Studying how presence is formed, broken, and recovered.

**Human-Centered AI**
Intelligent systems that read and align with human experience in real time.

**Game Technology**
Games as laboratory and muse, powered by real-time 3D and neural rendering.

## Themes

| | |
|---|---|
| **XR Presence & AI Interpretation** | We measure gaze, motion, and behavior, and train AI models to read how presence is formed, broken, and recovered. |
| **Adaptive XR with AI** | Virtual worlds that sense the user and let AI adjust them in real time, keeping immersion high and discomfort low. |
| **Social & Multi-User XR** | How people work, play, and recover together in shared virtual spaces, and how AI can mediate what each person sees and when to step in. |
| **Game Technology & AI Rendering** | Real-time 3D worlds built and rendered with AI, with games as both our laboratory and our inspiration. |
